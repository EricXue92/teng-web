Join Us

We welcome enquiries from prospective PhD and MPhil students, research assistants and visiting scholars with a background in construction management, civil engineering, environmental science or related fields. Experience in life-cycle assessment, optimisation or building information modelling is an advantage.

Please send a CV, transcripts and a short statement of research interests to yue.teng@polyu.edu.hk. Information on PolyU research postgraduate admissions and the Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme is available on the Graduate School website.
