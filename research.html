Research

Our work asks a practical question: how can buildings be designed, built and reused with the least carbon and waste? We answer it with life-cycle assessment, optimisation models and digital platforms, and we test the results with industry partners in Hong Kong and mainland China.

Research Interests

Construction Carbon Management & Optimization

Work on this theme addresses the assessment, allocation and reduction of embodied carbon across material production, component manufacturing, transportation, on-site construction, demolition and recovery. Particular attention is given to modular and prefabricated construction. Research examines how supply chain coordination, design and construction optimization, and circular construction practices can improve carbon performance.

Carbon Information Technologies & AI

Information technologies and artificial intelligence methods are developed for collecting, calculating, tracking, verifying and predicting carbon emissions. These methods connect building models, IoT data and analytical algorithms to support the digital representation, continuous updating and trusted exchange of carbon information. The emphasis is on the underlying technologies and computational methods.

Carbon Policy, Markets & Urban Resilience

The focus here is on the policy instruments, market mechanisms and urban systems that shape the construction sector’s low-carbon transition. Studies investigate how incentives influence emission reduction, how carbon emissions vary across cities and regions, and how urban infrastructure can adapt to external disruptions. Research also considers how resilience objectives can be aligned with decarbonization.

Building Energy & Operational Carbon

Energy consumption and carbon emissions during building operation are at the core of this area. It examines building energy performance, retrofit strategies and renewable energy integration in relation to building characteristics, occupant requirements and energy system conditions. Research identifies the principal sources of operational emissions and evaluates the reduction potential of alternative technologies and operating strategies.

Zero Carbon Buildings

We develop methods to measure and reduce embodied and whole-life carbon of buildings. Topics include system boundaries of life-cycle carbon assessment, the influence of LCA database selection on results, end-of-life and reuse scenarios, and how to allocate carbon responsibility among stakeholders in the construction supply chain.

Prefabrication

Prefabricated construction changes where carbon is emitted and who controls it. We quantify the carbon and cost of prefabricated components from cradle to end-of-use, and study how design, logistics and contract arrangements shape the outcome, including profit distribution across the supply chain.

Prefabricated componentsSupply chain Cradle to end-of-useGame theory

Modular Integrated Construction (MiC)

MiC moves most construction into the factory. We investigate how it reduces construction waste, how to schedule work packages to minimise both carbon and cost, how to model constraints in on-site assembly, and how blockchain, IoT and BIM can provide real-time carbon monitoring for modular projects.
