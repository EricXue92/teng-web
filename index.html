Yue TENG

Assistant Professor · PhD (HKU) · MRICS
Department of Building and Real Estate, The Hong Kong Polytechnic University

I study how the built environment can reach net zero. My research focuses on the life-cycle carbon of buildings, prefabrication and modular integrated construction (MiC), combining life-cycle assessment, optimisation and digital tools to cut emissions across the construction supply chain.
