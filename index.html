Research Interests

Read more →

Construction Carbon Management & Optimization

This research area addresses the assessment, allocation and reduction of embodied carbon across material production, component manufacturing, transportation, on-site construction, demolition and recovery. Particular attention is given to modular and prefabricated construction. Research examines how supply chain coordination, design and construction optimization, and circular construction practices can improve carbon performance.

Carbon Information Technologies & AI

This research area develops information technologies and artificial intelligence methods for collecting, calculating, tracking, verifying and predicting carbon emissions. It connects building models, IoT data and analytical algorithms to support the digital representation, continuous updating and trusted exchange of carbon information. The emphasis is on the underlying technologies and computational methods.

Carbon Policy, Markets & Urban Resilience

This research area examines the policy instruments, market mechanisms and urban systems that shape the construction sector’s low-carbon transition. It investigates how incentives influence emission reduction, how carbon emissions vary across cities and regions, and how urban infrastructure can adapt to external disruptions. Research also considers how resilience objectives can be aligned with decarbonization.

Building Energy & Operational Carbon

This research area focuses on energy consumption and carbon emissions during building operation. It examines building energy performance, retrofit strategies and renewable energy integration in relation to building characteristics, occupant requirements and energy system conditions. Research identifies the principal sources of operational emissions and evaluates the reduction potential of alternative technologies and operating strategies.
