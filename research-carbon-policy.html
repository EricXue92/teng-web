Carbon Policy, Markets & Urban Resilience

Develop a total factor carbon (TFC) framework that adopts a total factor perspective on building projects. Provide a quantitative tool that helps stakeholders make informed decisions on efficient and actionable carbon reduction strategies from a comprehensive, multidimensional perspective.

Leverage blockchain-based smart contracts and consensus mechanisms to automate participation in carbon markets and incentivize participants to reduce emissions.

Conduct a systematic literature review on MiC and carbon credit mechanisms to synthesize existing research themes and identify the key challenges and enabling pathways for MiC carbon credit development. Propose a preliminary indicator-based diagnostic framework to translate these findings into observable and measurable indicators.
